Privacy Policy

Sokoban - Over 3000+ hard puzzles is a single-player puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

Last updated: 23 October 2026 Publisher: Indie Apps Applies to: Microsoft Store and desktop versions

The short version: no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Game progress and settings stay on your own device. The only optional purchase is a one-time Store add-on that unlocks unlimited hints.

What the app stores

The app stores game data only on your own device, using the local storage of its app sandbox:

  • Which puzzles you have completed, with your best move and push counts
  • The position, moves and timer of the puzzle you are currently playing, so the game can offer to resume it
  • Your settings: music and sound volume, mute, dark or light theme, and reduced-motion preference
  • Which puzzles have used a hint, and a local cache of whether the optional unlimited-hints add-on is active

This data never leaves your computer. It is not uploaded, synced or read by anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it. You can also erase progress and hint records from the app's settings or by clearing the app's local data.

Optional in-app purchase

The Microsoft Store version offers one optional durable add-on, All Hints Unlock. The first 10 hints on 10 different puzzles are free; re-opening a hint on a puzzle that has already used one stays free. The add-on removes the hint limit permanently.

  • All 3,332 puzzles remain free and playable without the add-on.
  • The transaction and the localized price are handled by the Microsoft Store.
  • The app asks Windows for the add-on license state and price. It does not receive your name, email address, payment method or other Microsoft account data.
  • The local record of the purchase is only a cache. The Microsoft Store license is the source of truth, so a purchase can be restored after reinstalling or on another device signed in to the same Microsoft account.

Network access

The app declares no network capability in its package manifest and does not make its own network requests. Microsoft Store license checks and purchases are brokered by Windows Store services; the app only receives the resulting license state and localized price string.

Microsoft processes Store transactions under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no social features, no user-generated content and no external links inside the game.

Third-party components

The app bundles open-source and Creative Commons game assets, including level data, pixel art, music and sound effects. None of these components collect or transmit data. Full attribution is available in the in-game About screen.
